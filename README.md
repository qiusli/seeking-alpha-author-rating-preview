# Seeking Alpha Article Research Preview

An unpacked Chrome, Edge, or Helium extension for quicker research from a Seeking Alpha article page.

- Hover **Rating history** beside the author to preview the author's past ticker ratings and price history.
- Hover **Financials** to preview the article ticker's income statement, balance sheet, and cash-flow statement. Choose Quarterly or Annual, resize the panel, and use the fixed horizontal scrollbar for hidden columns.

Seeking Alpha data is requested from the active signed-in page; browser cookies are neither copied nor stored. Price lines come from Yahoo Finance's public chart endpoint.

## Install

1. Open your browser's extensions page.
2. Enable Developer mode.
3. Choose **Load unpacked** and select this folder.
4. Open or refresh a `seekingalpha.com/article/...` page.
